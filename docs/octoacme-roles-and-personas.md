# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, ensuring features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify and triage defects with clear reproduction steps
- Validate releases meet quality gates before production deployment
- Advocate for test coverage and quality metrics

### Goals
- Reduce defects in production
- Increase confidence in release readiness
- Enable fast feedback loops during development

### Typical Communication
- Weekly syncs with PM and delivery team
- Test case documentation and defect reports
- Pre-release quality sign-off

---

## Technical Architect/Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define solution design, technology choices, and scalability strategies to support project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and integration points
- Advise on technology choices and trade-offs
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Review design docs and technical proposals
- Mentor developers on best practices

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and reliable
- Reduce technical debt
- Align technical decisions with business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk register updates for technical risks
- Code review feedback and mentoring

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve decisions, and ensure alignment with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success metrics
- Approve project charter and release decisions
- Communicate project status to leadership
- Provide feedback on priorities and trade-offs
- Resolve scope and resource conflicts

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain organizational alignment
- Enable rapid decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Decision approvals on scope and timeline changes
- Executive status reports

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure products are usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs and brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design practices
- Review implementation for design fidelity
- Iterate on design based on user feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centric experiences
- Reduce support burden through good design
- Increase user satisfaction and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Acceptance criteria refinement
- Usability testing reports

---

## DevOps/Operations Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and operational processes to enable reliable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure and cloud resources
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Support incident response and rollback procedures
- Optimize for reliability, security, and cost

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments
- Minimize downtime and incidents
- Ensure infrastructure supports business growth

### Typical Communication
- Release coordination and deployment planning
- Infrastructure and operational risk reviews
- Post-incident retrospectives

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove blockers, and help teams optimize their delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Help teams identify and remove blockers
- Coach teams on agile practices and ceremonies
- Track and communicate team velocity and capacity
- Support continuous process improvement

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and throughput
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Reduce waste and cycle time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Process improvement recommendations

---

## How These Personas Interact

- **Developers** collaborate with QA/Testing Leads for acceptance validation, Technical Architects for design guidance, UX/Design Leads for implementation fidelity, and DevOps Engineers for deployment support.
- **Product Managers** work with Stakeholders/Sponsors for priority alignment, UX/Design Leads for user research, Developers for estimation and feasibility, and QA/Testing Leads for acceptance criteria.
- **Project Managers** coordinate across all roles to ensure delivery on schedule and within scope, managing dependencies and communication.
- **Technical Architects** advise Developers and DevOps on design and infrastructure decisions, and work with Project Managers to identify and mitigate technical risks.
- **QA/Testing Leads** validate work from Developers against acceptance criteria, coordinate pre-release activities with DevOps, and partner with Product Managers on quality standards.
- **UX/Design Leads** partner with Product Managers on feature definition and user research, work with Developers on implementation fidelity, and validate design quality.
- **Stakeholders/Sponsors** provide business context and decisions to Product Managers and Project Managers, and receive regular updates on progress.
- **DevOps Engineers** support Developers and QA with deployment and infrastructure needs, coordinate release activities with Project Managers, and manage operational risks.
- **Scrum Masters** facilitate team ceremonies and remove blockers across all roles, helping the team optimize delivery velocity and process.
